Program

Service & Support Dogs

Helping provide trained service dogs to veterans and emotional support dogs to spouses and children of fallen service members.

What we are building

Our goal is to create pathways to greater independence, companionship, comfort, and support through carefully developed partnerships and assistance programs.

Veterans

Support will focus on trained service dogs that help eligible veterans address service-connected needs and increase independence.

Families of the Fallen

We also intend to help spouses and children of fallen service members access emotional support dogs where appropriate.

Program details, eligibility requirements, and application procedures will be announced as this program is formally established.

Forged to Serve Foundation

Supporting veterans and the families of fallen service members through meaningful assistance, resources, and opportunity.
