Program

Service Dogs for Veterans

Helping eligible veterans access professionally trained service dogs that support greater independence, safety, and quality of life.

Planned veteran support

The program is being developed to support veterans whose needs may include PTSD-related tasks, mobility assistance, TBI-related support, and other appropriate service-dog work.

  • Professionally trained service dogs
  • Veteran-and-dog placement support
  • Public-access and task training
  • Training equipment
  • Veterinary care support
  • Ongoing follow-up and partnership support

Support dogs for families

Forged to Serve also intends to develop support-dog assistance for eligible spouses and children of fallen service members. The exact program model and terminology will be published once formally approved.

Program details, eligibility requirements, partner standards, and application procedures will be announced as the program is formally established.
