Families of the Fallen

Planned support may include emergency financial assistance, family resources, holiday and special-event assistance, support-dog assistance, community outreach, memorial and remembrance initiatives, and connections to additional veteran and family resources.

Veteran-Owned Small Businesses

Planned assistance may include business startup support, professional services, mentorship, networking, business education, marketing and development resources, and equipment or technology assistance.

Service Dogs for Veterans

Planned assistance may include service-dog training, veteran-and-dog placement support, training equipment, veterinary care, public-access and task training, and ongoing follow-up support.

Programs are in development. Eligibility, application procedures, award criteria, and availability will be published before assistance is formally offered.
