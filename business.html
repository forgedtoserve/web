Veteran Small Business Assistance

Helping veteran entrepreneurs turn experience, ideas, and determination into stronger, more sustainable businesses.

Planned areas of assistance

  • Business startup assistance
  • Professional services and technical guidance
  • Mentorship and networking opportunities
  • Business education and training
  • Marketing and development resources
  • Equipment or technology assistance as funding permits
  • Financial assistance opportunities under written program criteria

Built for practical impact

The goal is not simply to provide information, but to connect eligible veteran entrepreneurs with resources and support that can help them move a business forward.

Specific award criteria, funding limits, documentation requirements, and application procedures will be published before financial assistance becomes available.
