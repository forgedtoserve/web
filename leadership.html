Leadership

Board of Directors

Forged to Serve is guided by a volunteer board committed to responsible governance, practical service, and meaningful support for veterans and families.

RH

Richard Hacker

President

Richard serves as President of Forged to Serve Foundation and helps lead organizational development, board governance, program planning, partnerships, and the foundation’s overall direction.

VH

Vanessa Carraco-Hacker

Vice President / Treasurer

Vanessa serves as Vice President and Treasurer, supporting the board’s work in organizational development, financial stewardship, program planning, donor engagement, and community partnership development.

SL

Scott F. Lowry

Board Member

Scott F. Lowry is a U.S. Army veteran with nearly 30 years of experience in information technology, leadership, nonprofit service, veteran outreach, program development, and community partnerships.

He brings experience in operations, technology, veteran advocacy, nonprofit leadership, and building community-focused programs to the board.

NI

Nicole Ingraham

Secretary / Board Member

Nicole serves as Secretary and Board Member, supporting board records, meeting documentation, organizational development, and the foundation’s governance work.

Building the organization responsibly

The Board of Directors is currently developing the foundation’s bylaws, program criteria, donation procedures, procurement practices, sponsorship opportunities, and operating framework. Additional leadership biographies and photos will be added as they are finalized for publication.

Governance

Accountability from the beginning.

The board is working to clearly define officer responsibilities, establish written program procedures, and build a transparent foundation for future operations.
