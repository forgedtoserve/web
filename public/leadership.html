Board of Directors

Forged to Serve is guided by a volunteer board committed to responsible governance, practical service, and meaningful support for veterans and families.

Founder & Executive Leadership

Richard Hacker

Founder, President & Executive Director

Richard Hacker is the Founder, President, and Executive Director of Forged to Serve Foundation. He served five years in the U.S. Army Infantry, including multiple deployments, and held leadership positions as a Team Leader and Weapons Squad Leader. His military experience shaped his commitment to leadership, service, and supporting those who have served.

Richard founded Forged to Serve Foundation because he wanted to continue serving veterans and give back to the men and women who served our country. His goal is to help veterans and their families receive the support, resources, and opportunities they need to build stronger futures.

Why I Serve

After serving five years in the infantry and experiencing deployments and the bond that comes from serving alongside others, I believe the responsibility to serve doesn’t end when you take off the uniform. I want to continue serving veterans and their families, give back to those who served, and make sure they know they are never forgotten or alone.

Business & Community Leadership

Vanessa Hacker

Vice President / Treasurer

For Vanessa Hacker, Forged to Serve is about standing beside the people who have spent their lives standing for others. As Richard’s partner in life and in this mission, she saw firsthand the challenges that inspired him to create the foundation and understood that the vision would need people willing to build it, support it, and bring the community together.

Vanessa brings experience in business, real estate, property management, accounting, and community relationships. As Vice President and Treasurer, she helps provide the organization, leadership, and financial stewardship needed to turn the foundation’s mission into responsible, sustainable action.

She believes supporting veterans means looking beyond a service record and recognizing the person and family behind the uniform. Her focus is helping Forged to Serve build strong community relationships, operate responsibly, and create support that veterans and their families can trust.

Why I Serve

“Service shouldn’t end when someone takes off the uniform. We believe our responsibility to our veterans continues long after their service does.”

Veteran & Program Leadership

Scott F. Lowry

Program Director

Scott F. Lowry is a United States Army veteran who served nearly 18 years, including multiple deployments. His military service helped shape a lifelong commitment to leadership, service, and supporting fellow veterans and their families.

Following his military career, Scott built nearly 30 years of experience in information technology, operations, nonprofit leadership, and community service. Over the years, he has remained heavily involved in veteran advocacy and programs designed to strengthen connections between veterans, families, and the communities around them.

Scott currently serves as Program Director for Forged to Serve Foundation, where he helps develop and coordinate programs focused on veterans and the families of fallen service members. His work includes helping turn the foundation’s mission into practical programs, building community partnerships, identifying resources, and creating opportunities that can make a meaningful difference in people’s lives.

Scott believes effective veteran support requires more than good intentions. It takes strong programs, trusted relationships, responsible stewardship, and a willingness to listen to what veterans and families actually need.

Through Forged to Serve, Scott is committed to helping build an organization that serves with purpose, operates with integrity, and ensures veterans and families know there is a community ready to stand beside them.

Why I Serve

Scott joined Forged to Serve to help build practical support systems that turn good intentions into meaningful assistance for veterans and the families of fallen service members. He brings experience in veteran advocacy, nonprofit leadership, program development, technology, operations, and community partnerships to help the foundation build responsibly and serve effectively.

Service Dogs & Outreach

Nicole Ingraham

Secretary, Paws4Patriots Director & Outreach Coordinator

Nicole Ingraham has dedicated her career to service dogs, service, and giving back to the community, with a particular passion for supporting veterans and their families. Her connection to the veteran community is deeply personal: her grandfather and brother are veterans, and she grew up in a family that believes in honoring and standing behind those who have served.

Nicole has worked closely with veterans for more than three years and has spent over a decade training service dogs. She also owns and operates Golden Heart Service Dogs, LLC, where she specializes in service-dog training. Before joining Forged to Serve, she spent several years with another veteran nonprofit, gaining experience in veteran outreach, service-dog programs, and community support.

At Forged to Serve, Nicole helps develop veteran-focused programs, build community relationships, coordinate outreach, and connect veterans with resources and opportunities that can genuinely improve their lives. She is especially excited to help build the organization from the ground up and create programs with lasting impact.

Why I Serve

“We owe so much to the men and women who have served our country, and giving back to them is one small way we can show our gratitude.”

Leadership built for the mission

Our leadership team brings together military service, program development, community outreach, service-dog expertise, business experience, financial stewardship, and nonprofit leadership. Together, we are building Forged to Serve around clear standards, responsible governance, and programs designed to create lasting impact for veterans and the families of fallen service members.

Governance

Accountability in every decision.

Forged to Serve is committed to transparent leadership, responsible financial stewardship, clear program standards, and thoughtful use of every resource entrusted to the foundation. Our board and program leaders work together to keep the mission focused, measurable, and worthy of the veterans and families we serve.
