United for Warriors Poker Run and Concert

A full day of community, veteran support, family activities, food, fundraising, a motorcycle poker run, and live music — all coming together to support veterans and their families.

One Community. One Mission.

More than a concert.

United for Warriors Poker Run and Concert is being planned as a community-wide day that connects veterans and families with resources, brings local organizations and businesses together, and raises support for the veteran community.

The event is a joint effort involving Forged to Serve Foundation, VFW Post 9126, Warhound Veteran Motorcycle Association, Broken Warrior Ranch, and Everybody Eats of Tulsa.

Veteran Resources

Veterans Row

Veteran-serving nonprofits and resource organizations will be invited to participate in a dedicated Veterans Row. The current plan is to provide approved veteran resource booths at no charge so organizations can focus on connecting veterans and families with useful services and support.

21+ Area

Adult Beverage Area

A designated adult beverage area is planned for guests age 21 and older. Alcohol service is expected to be handled by a properly licensed and insured mobile bar operator with controlled access to the service area.

Final beverage-area layout, access control, licensing, and operating details remain subject to event planning and applicable requirements.
