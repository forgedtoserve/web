Veteran Small Business Assistance

Helping veteran entrepreneurs turn experience, ideas, and determination into stronger, more sustainable businesses.

Built for Practical Impact

Helping veterans move good ideas forward.

The goal is not simply to provide information, but to connect eligible veteran entrepreneurs with resources and support that can help them start, strengthen, and grow a business.

Planned Assistance

Resources for the work behind the business.

Our planned support focuses on the practical needs veteran entrepreneurs face while building and operating a business.

🚀

Startup Assistance

Business startup assistance for eligible veterans developing or launching a new venture.

🛠️

Professional Services

Professional services and technical guidance that help solve operational and business challenges.

🤝

Mentorship & Networking

Mentorship and networking opportunities that connect veteran entrepreneurs with experience and community.

📚

Education & Training

Business education and training focused on practical skills and sustainable growth.

📣

Marketing & Development

Marketing and development resources that can help veteran-owned businesses strengthen their presence.

💻

Equipment & Technology

Equipment or technology assistance as funding permits and written program criteria allow.

Financial Assistance

Financial assistance opportunities under written program criteria as funding becomes available.

Program Standards

Support with clear criteria.
