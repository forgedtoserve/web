What We Do

Purpose-built support where it matters most.

Our programs focus on three areas where targeted support can create lasting impact for veterans and military families.

01Veterans

Service Dogs for Veterans

Helping eligible veterans access professionally trained service dogs, while developing support-dog assistance for spouses and children of fallen service members.

02Opportunity

Why We Serve

The mission continues after the uniform comes off.

Our responsibility to veterans—and to the families who sacrificed alongside them—does not end when military service does.

Forged to Serve was created to turn gratitude into action: connecting people with resources, building partnerships, and creating practical ways to support those who served and the families who carry their legacy forward.
