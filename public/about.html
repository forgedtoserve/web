About Us

Forged by Service. Driven to Serve.

Forged to Serve Foundation exists to support veterans and the spouses and children of fallen service members through practical assistance, meaningful opportunities, and strong community partnerships.

Who We Are

Built around service, dignity, and practical support.

Our work is centered on helping people move forward with resources that make a real difference. We are building programs designed to strengthen independence, create opportunity, honor sacrifice, and connect veterans and families with communities that are ready to stand beside them.

What Guides Us

Service that turns into action.

Our approach is grounded in clear priorities, responsible stewardship, and a commitment to build programs that are sustainable and worthy of the people they are intended to serve.

🇺🇸

Honor Service

We honor military service and sacrifice by building programs that provide meaningful, respectful support.

🤝

Build Connection

We bring together veterans, families, community partners, volunteers, and sponsors around shared purpose.

🧭

Provide Practical Help

Our goal is to create assistance that addresses real needs and supports stronger, more independent futures.

🛡️

Act Responsibly

Programs are being developed with written criteria, responsible review processes, and thoughtful stewardship.

⭐

Honor the Fallen

We remain committed to supporting the spouses and children of fallen service members while honoring their loved one's sacrifice.

📈

Create Opportunity

We invest in pathways that can strengthen independence, including veteran entrepreneurship and small-business support.

Our Mission in Practice

Three core areas of focus.

Forged to Serve Foundation is currently building programs around service dogs for veterans, veteran small-business assistance, and support for the spouses and children of fallen service members.

Program details, eligibility requirements, application procedures, and available forms of assistance will be published as each program is formally established.
