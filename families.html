Program

Families of the Fallen

Supporting the spouses and children of fallen service members with the care, resources, and opportunities they deserve.

The sacrifice of a service member is also carried by the family left behind. Forged to Serve Foundation is building a program designed to provide compassionate, practical support while honoring the service and sacrifice of their loved one.

Planned areas of support

  • Emergency financial assistance
  • Family support resources and referrals
  • Holiday and special-event assistance
  • Support-dog assistance as the program model is finalized
  • Community outreach and connection
  • Memorial and remembrance initiatives
  • Connections to additional veteran and family resources

Support with dignity

Assistance will be guided by written charitable criteria, available funding, and a review process designed to provide meaningful support fairly and responsibly.

Eligibility requirements, application procedures, and available forms of assistance will be published before the program begins accepting requests.
