Program

Families of the Fallen

Supporting the spouses and children of fallen service members with the care, resources, and opportunities they deserve.

Our Commitment

Standing with the families who carried the sacrifice.

The sacrifice of a service member is also carried by the family left behind. Forged to Serve Foundation is building a program designed to provide compassionate, practical support while honoring the service and sacrifice of their loved one.

Planned Support

Practical help. Lasting connection.

Our planned areas of support are designed to meet families where they are and connect them with meaningful assistance.

💵

Emergency Assistance

Emergency financial assistance when an eligible family is facing an immediate need.

🧭

Family Resources

Family support resources, referrals, and connections to additional veteran and family services.

🎁

Holiday & Special Events

Holiday and special-event assistance intended to help families stay connected and supported.

🐕

Support-Dog Assistance

Support-dog assistance as the family program model and terminology are formally finalized.

🤝

Community Connection

Community outreach and connection that help families build supportive relationships and networks.

⭐

Remembrance

Memorial and remembrance initiatives that honor the service and sacrifice of fallen service members.

Support With Dignity

Fair, responsible, and mission-focused.

Assistance will be guided by written charitable criteria, available funding, and a review process designed to provide meaningful support fairly and responsibly.

Eligibility requirements, application procedures, and available forms of assistance will be published before the program begins accepting requests.

Have a question about this program?

We welcome inquiries from families, community partners, and organizations interested in supporting this mission area.
